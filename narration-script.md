# Taksir — Demo Video Narration Script

Video: `demo-video.mp4` (silent, 67 seconds, 1280×720).
Read time at a natural pace: ~60 seconds — comfortably under the
3-minute Devpost limit. Timestamps are approximate (±3 s); they follow
the sections of the recorded walkthrough against the live site
https://jaw1jennaira.github.io/taksir/.

---

## 0:00–0:05 — Home (empty store)

> "This is Taksir — a tool for small second-hand laptop sellers in
> Indonesia. It answers one question: what is a fair price for this
> laptop? No account, no sign-up — it works offline, right in the
> browser."

## 0:05–0:15 — Seller settings

> "First, the seller sets up their shop: store name, seller name, city,
> and a WhatsApp number. That number is what buyers will use to order
> later."

## 0:15–0:27 — Add a laptop with a photo

> "Now let's add a laptop: a Lenovo ThinkPad T480 — Core i5, 8
> gigabytes of RAM, 256 gigabyte SSD, battery health 82 percent, good
> physical condition, with charger. We upload a photo, and the app
> downsizes it automatically so it fits on the device."

## 0:27–0:43 — Transparent estimate

> "Here's the estimate: 5,600,000 rupiah. And this is the point of
> Taksir — the price is not a black box. Every adjustment is shown:
> the base price from the closest comparable listings, then CPU, RAM,
> storage, battery, condition, age, and accessories — each with its
> percentage and its reason. Below, the five most similar listings it
> was compared against. The app is honest about what it is: an estimate
> from reference data — not AI, not a live market price."

## 0:43–0:55 — Listing text, Indonesian and English

> "One tap generates the listing text — in Indonesian, and in English —
> ready to copy-paste to Facebook, WhatsApp, or Marketplace. The price
> is always written as an estimate, never as a guaranteed market price."

## 0:55–0:59 — Saved to inventory

> "The laptop is saved to the seller's inventory, with its estimate
> and availability status."

## 0:59–1:07 — Storefront and WhatsApp order

> "And here is the public storefront: the shop name, the available
> laptops, and a WhatsApp button on every card, pre-filled with the
> item and its estimated price — so a buyer can ask about it in one
> tap. That's Taksir: estimate transparently, list in two languages,
> and sell from your own storefront."

# Product — Taksir

## What it is

Taksir helps small second-hand sellers — starting with laptop sellers
in Indonesia, like our own user — answer the hardest question in their
trade: **"berapa harga yang wajar untuk barang ini?"** (what is a fair
price for this item?)

The seller uploads a photo, fills in the condition (brand, model, CPU,
RAM, storage, battery, cosmetic condition, accessories, age), and gets:

1. A price estimate with a **transparent breakdown** — every rupiah
   explained, every comparable shown.
2. Listing text in Indonesian **and** English, ready to copy-paste.
3. A public storefront page for their shop.
4. A WhatsApp order button with a prefilled message.

## Audience

- Primary: small second-hand laptop sellers in Indonesia (Tasikmalaya
  and similar cities) who sell through Facebook groups, WhatsApp, and
  Marketplace — not through big e-commerce platforms.
- Secondary: any second-hand seller who needs a defensible price and
  a fast listing, not a marketplace account.

## Problem

Sellers price by gut feel or by copying one random listing. Buyers
distrust the price, negotiations stall, and good stock sits unsold.
Existing "price check" tools either hide their method or pretend to
know the live market. A small seller needs something they can
**show the buyer**: "here is why this price is fair."

## Honesty is the product

Taksir's pricing engine is a **transparent rule-based scoring model**
over a reference comparables dataset. It is NOT machine learning, NOT
AI vision, NOT live market scraping. The app says so on every screen
that shows a price, and the dataset is labelled sample data the seller
can edit or replace. This honesty is deliberate: it is what makes the
estimate *showable* to a buyer, and it is what the hackathon's
Frankenstein category rewards — stitching a pricing tool, a listing
generator, and a storefront into one offline-first tool for sellers
who have never had any of them.

## Non-goals (v1)

- No payments, no checkout, no escrow — the deal closes on WhatsApp.
- No accounts, no backend, no tracking — all data stays on the device.
- No live market data — the reference dataset is the seller's own.
- Laptops first; other categories only after v1 ships.
